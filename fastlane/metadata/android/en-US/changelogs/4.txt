New terminal-first home screen: one open distro, a terminal with tabs, and a launcher-style apps grid.

- Remote access: SSH into a distro over your LAN or via a relay
- Clipboard: wl-copy/wl-paste work in the terminal
- Optional Keep awake so guests don't stall when the screen is off
- Graphics start on demand; one notification shows what's running
- Chromium and Electron apps run natively on Wayland
- Bundled Hack font and a terminal scale setting
- Smaller APK
