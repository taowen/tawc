sshyeet session-id and secret words

The RFC 1751 dictionary ("A Convention for Human-Readable 128-bit Keys",
D. McDonald, 1994; https://www.rfc-editor.org/rfc/rfc1751): 2048 words,
used by sshyeet relays for session ids and by TAWC for login secrets
(remote/src/sid/rfc1751.rs).

The 256 adjectives and 256 nouns of earlier sshyeet relays' ids
(https://sshyeet.com/dl/src.tar.gz, internal/sid/words.go), copied verbatim
so TAWC accepts the ids such relays assign (remote/src/sid/legacy_words.rs).
Used with the author's permission.
